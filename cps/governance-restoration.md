## Short Summary

This proposal seeks to exercise the governance-restoration mechanism expressly preserved under CP171 and reinstate meaningful Centrifuge DAO governance before any fundamental restructuring of CFG governance, value accrual, or ownership proceeds.

CP171 transferred operational authority to the Centrifuge Network Foundation and Centrifuge Labs while explicitly preserving ultimate control for CFG tokenholders. It stated that tokenholders would retain the ability to create a proposal to transfer control back to the DAO and described the arrangement as reversible by design.

This proposal invokes that safeguard.

## High-level objective

The objective is to restore DAO governance so CFG holders can meaningfully participate in decisions that materially affect the governance, economic rights, value-accrual framework, treasury, and future structure of Centrifuge.

This proposal is not intended to oppose Centrifuge Labs, the Centrifuge Network Foundation, tokenized equity, or the strategic direction contemplated by CP172.

It is intended to ensure that any such fundamental decision is considered under the governance protections expressly retained by CFG holders under CP171.

## Background

CP171 materially changed Centrifuge governance by pausing active DAO governance mechanisms and transferring strategy and execution to Centrifuge Labs under Centrifuge Network Foundation oversight.

At the same time, CP171 expressly preserved ultimate authority for CFG holders.

CP171 states:

- Tokenholders retain ultimate control under the structure.
- If tokenholders want to reinstate the DAO, they retain the ability to create a proposal to transfer control back to the DAO.
- The arrangement is reversible by design.
- The DAO may choose to reclaim operational or treasury control through future governance processes.

CP171 also established CFG as the single value-accrual mechanism and stated that there was no equity business.

CP172 subsequently proposes exploring a token-to-equity structure that could fundamentally alter the governance and economic framework established under CP171.

Given the significance of that potential restructuring, this proposal asks the community to first determine whether it wishes to exercise the governance-restoration right preserved under CP171.

## Description of activity

If approved, this proposal would:

1. Reinstate meaningful DAO governance mechanisms for CFG holders.

2. Restore the ability of CFG holders to introduce, debate, amend and vote on governance proposals affecting the Centrifuge protocol, treasury, governance framework and CFG value-accrual structure.

3. Restore the governance authority contemplated by CP171's explicit safeguard allowing tokenholders to transfer control back to the DAO.

4. Require material proposals concerning a fundamental restructuring of CFG governance, ownership or value accrual to proceed through restored DAO governance.

5. Preserve the ability of Centrifuge Labs and the Centrifuge Network Foundation to continue ordinary operational execution while the community governance process is restored.

## Relationship to CP172

This proposal does not seek to predetermine the outcome of CP172.

CFG holders may ultimately support tokenized equity or another restructuring model.

The purpose of this proposal is procedural and governance-focused: before CFG holders are asked to approve a fundamental change to the framework established under CP171, they should first have the opportunity to exercise the governance rights CP171 expressly preserved.

Accordingly, the community should determine whether DAO governance should be reinstated before any irreversible implementation of a restructuring that materially changes CFG governance or value accrual.

## Change or improvement

The proposed improvement is the restoration of meaningful community governance at a point where the protocol is considering a potentially fundamental structural change.

Restoring governance would:

- Give CFG holders a meaningful voice in decisions affecting their economic and governance interests.
- Provide a transparent process for considering major structural changes.
- Give practical effect to the safeguard expressly preserved under CP171.
- Improve legitimacy and community confidence in any eventual decision concerning CP172 or similar proposals.
- Preserve operational execution while restoring oversight for fundamental governance decisions.

## Alignment with the Centrifuge mission

Centrifuge was built as open infrastructure for real-world assets.

Transparent and meaningful governance is consistent with that mission.

The purpose of decentralised governance is not to prevent teams from executing effectively. It is to ensure that stakeholders retain meaningful agency over fundamental changes to the systems they helped build and support.

Restoring DAO governance under the mechanism expressly preserved in CP171 would demonstrate that Centrifuge's governance commitments remain meaningful even when difficult or consequential decisions arise.

## Governance process

This proposal should proceed through the governance process applicable under the Centrifuge Proposal Framework.

Following the required RFC discussion period and repository review, CFG holders should be given the opportunity to vote on whether DAO governance should be reinstated pursuant to CP171.
